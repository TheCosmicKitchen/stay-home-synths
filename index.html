Tips

  • Drag knobs up/down (or sideways). Double-tap a knob to reset it.
  • Tap the scope at the top to switch to a spectrum view.
  • Lower on a key = louder. Hold latches notes so you can tweak knobs with both hands.
  • Computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; ' play notes, Z/X octave, Space drums.
  • MIDI keyboards and controllers work in Chrome, Edge and Firefox (not Safari). Connect them in the MIDI panel above.
  • Everything you change is saved in this browser automatically.
